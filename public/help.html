Getting started

Training system: Use only demo guest and payment information provided by your instructor. Never enter a real card number or sensitive personal information.
  1. 1
    Sign in as your assigned operator.

    Select the employee and shift given by your instructor. Visible tabs depend on the employee’s role.

  2. 2
    Confirm your context.

    Check the business date, shift, operator name, and role before posting anything.

  3. 3
    Review the dashboard.

    Look at arrivals, departures, room issues, occupied rooms, and open folios.

  4. 4
    Use the correct module.

    Reservations manages bookings; Room Rack shows readiness; Services posts charges; Cashiering settles folios.

Reservations

Create and review a reservation

  1. Open Reservations or select New Reservation.
  2. Enter the guest name, nationality, ID details, contact information, and stay dates.
  3. Select room type, occupants, rate plan, nightly rate, and guarantee method.
  4. Add relevant preferences or notes, then save.
  5. Check the confirmation number. Use Details to review and Print Confirmation when required.

Due In

Arrival is on the active business date. The guest may be assigned and checked in when the room is ready.

Reserved

Arrival is on a future date. Keep the booking for later processing.

Arrivals

Assign a room and check in

Assign the room

  1. Find a Due In reservation and select its assignment action.
  2. Confirm the room matches the requested type and is ready.
  3. Complete assignment. Occupancy changes from Vacant to Assigned.

Complete check-in

  1. Select Check In.
  2. Review identity, dates, room, rate, notes, and payment guarantee.
  3. Record payment method and keys issued.
  4. Confirm registration details and complete check-in.
System result: The reservation becomes Checked In, the room becomes Occupied, an in-house stay is created, and the initial room charge is posted.

Guest account

Folio, service charges, and payments

Post a service charge

  1. Open Services.
  2. Select the guest or room.
  3. Enter service, quantity, price, reference, and notes.
  4. Review the total and post.

Post a payment

  1. Open Cashiering.
  2. Select the unsettled folio.
  3. Review entries and balance.
  4. Post cash, mock card sale, or capture a mock authorization.
Authorization is not payment. It reserves an amount. Capturing it posts payment and reduces the balance.

Charges increase the balance; payments reduce it. Verify guest, room, amount, and reference before posting.

Departures

Complete guest check-out

  1. 1
    Open Departures

    Verify guest, room, and departure date.

  2. 2
    Review the folio

    Confirm all charges and payments.

  3. 3
    Settle any balance

    Check-out is blocked while money remains due.

  4. 4
    Complete check-out

    The room becomes Vacant and Dirty for housekeeping.

Room Rack

Understand room statuses

Every room has three separate statuses. Read all three before using it.

Occupancy

Vacant
No assigned or checked-in guest.
Assigned
Held for an arriving guest.
Occupied
Guest is checked in.

Housekeeping

Clean
Cleaned and potentially ready.
Inspected
Certified ready.
Dirty
Cleaning required.
Pickup
Minor service required.

Maintenance

In Service
May be used.
Out of Service
Temporarily unavailable.
Out of Order
Unavailable for a significant issue.

Accountability

Change shift and sign out

  1. Select Change Shift and review arrivals and unsettled folios.
  2. Select the incoming employee and shift.
  3. Write useful notes and confirm pending work and cashier responsibility were reviewed.
  4. Complete transfer. Tabs change to the incoming operator’s role.
End of work: Always use Sign Out. For safety, an operator session also signs out automatically after 30 minutes without activity.

Reports

View and print reports

Supervisors and Night Auditors can produce the Arrival Forecast, Room State Summary, Cashier Ledger, Services Summary, and Departure List.

Reports use currently loaded business-date data and open the browser print dialog. Review before printing or saving as PDF.

Troubleshooting

I cannot assign a room

Check that the booking is Due In; room type matches; and room is Vacant, Clean or Inspected, and In Service.

I cannot check in

Confirm a room is assigned to the same guest and remains ready and In Service.

I cannot check out

The folio balance must be exactly zero.

I cannot see a module or button

Check the operator role in the header. It may lack permission.

The same operator cannot sign in

The operator may still be active on another workstation. An abandoned session is released after 30 inactive minutes; otherwise, sign out on that workstation or ask your instructor.

Why was I returned to sign-in?

For safety, the system closes sessions after 30 minutes without keyboard, pointer, touch, scrolling, or operational activity. Sign in again to continue.

Another workstation’s change is missing

Refresh the browser; other open screens do not update automatically.
